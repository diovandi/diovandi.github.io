I work at the intersection of mechanical engineering and software. I am especially interested in computational design, automation, robotics, optimization, and the small practical details that turn an idea into something people can actually use.

This website is a living record of that work. I use it to document projects, share technical notes, and make the path from an experiment to a finished result more visible.

## What you will find here

- **Projects** — selected tools, experiments, and engineering work.
- **Blog** — technical notes, lessons learned, and occasional longer essays.
- **Work in progress** — honest documentation of ideas as they develop.
