# Portfolio content collection checklist

Use this as a collection queue. Do not wait until every box is complete before improving the site; one strong image and one credible result per project is enough for the first pass.

## Profile and site-wide material

- [ ] A clear headshot or working photo that you are comfortable publishing
- [ ] A one-sentence professional positioning statement
- [ ] Current CV or résumé PDF
- [ ] Preferred public email address
- [ ] GitHub, LinkedIn, ORCID, Google Scholar, and other relevant profile links
- [ ] Short biography (80–150 words)
- [ ] Longer biography covering interests, education, and current direction
- [ ] List of skills you can support with concrete project evidence

## Collect for every project

- [ ] Official project title, dates, organization, and location
- [ ] Your exact role and individual contribution
- [ ] Team size and collaborators who may be credited publicly
- [ ] The problem, requirement, or research question
- [ ] Two to five measurable results with units and test conditions
- [ ] Tools, software, hardware, and methods actually used
- [ ] One strong cover image
- [ ] Two to six supporting images: prototype, CAD, diagram, test setup, plot, or final result
- [ ] Descriptive captions explaining what each image proves
- [ ] Repository, demo, video, report, paper, poster, or presentation links
- [ ] Evidence of completion: test result, acceptance, award, launch, deployment, or delivered report
- [ ] Important limitations, failures, or unresolved work
- [ ] Permission to publish team, employer, university, or client material

## Engineering evidence

- [ ] Requirements or design criteria
- [ ] System architecture, block diagram, or workflow
- [ ] CAD render or annotated drawing
- [ ] Prototype and test-rig photographs
- [ ] Validation plot with labeled axes, units, legend, and sample count
- [ ] Baseline-versus-result comparison
- [ ] Error, tolerance, uncertainty, or sensitivity information
- [ ] Final design decision and the trade-off behind it
- [ ] Your code or calculation contribution, with confidential details removed

## Papers, reports, and research

- [ ] Final title and author order
- [ ] Publication type stated accurately: peer-reviewed, preprint, thesis, course paper, or technical report
- [ ] Venue, institution, year, DOI, or stable public URL
- [ ] Abstract or a concise problem–method–result summary
- [ ] Your individual contribution
- [ ] One representative figure or graphical abstract you may legally publish
- [ ] Key numerical result with necessary context
- [ ] Dataset and code availability
- [ ] Citation in BibTeX or another consistent format
- [ ] Known limitations and current publication status

## D03 morphing leading-edge project

- [ ] Final Group D03 paper PDF or a public link
- [ ] Confirmed author names and preferred spelling
- [ ] LeaTop demonstrator photograph with permission/source
- [ ] Photogrammetry comparison figure
- [ ] FEM displacement or strain visualization
- [ ] Experimental-versus-numerical strain plot
- [ ] Actuation-energy result or diagram
- [ ] One sentence describing your individual contribution
- [ ] Confirmation that the 7.73° result and 5° requirement may be shown publicly

## Image preparation

- [ ] Use original-resolution source files before exporting web copies
- [ ] Prefer `.webp` or optimized `.jpg` for photographs and `.png`/`.svg` for diagrams
- [ ] Export a consistent landscape cover crop, ideally around 1600 × 900
- [ ] Keep normal web images near or below 500 KB where quality permits
- [ ] Remove confidential labels, personal data, serial numbers, and location metadata
- [ ] Record creator, source, permission, and required credit for every non-original image
- [ ] Write useful alt text describing the image's informational purpose
- [ ] Use descriptive filenames such as `d03-photogrammetry-comparison.webp`

## Blog and credibility material

- [ ] Three possible technical article topics drawn from completed work
- [ ] One project retrospective explaining a difficult decision
- [ ] One validation or testing deep dive
- [ ] Awards, certificates, launch records, press coverage, or official announcements
- [ ] Testimonials or supervisor feedback that you have permission to quote
- [ ] Talks, posters, workshops, mentoring, and leadership activities

## Before publishing any item

- [ ] Claims match the underlying evidence
- [ ] Team work is not presented as individual work
- [ ] Publication status is labeled accurately
- [ ] Confidential and export-controlled information is excluded
- [ ] Every external asset has permission and attribution
- [ ] Links open without requiring a private account
- [ ] Names, dates, units, and numbers are consistent across the site and CV
