The KST Rating Tool applies Kinematic Screw Theory to evaluate mechanical assemblies. It brings the analysis into two practical interfaces: a command-line workflow for repeatable studies and a Fusion 360 add-in for use alongside CAD work.

## Why I built it

Mechanical assembly decisions are often distributed across geometry, constraints, fasteners, and manufacturing context. This project explores how those decisions can be evaluated more systematically while keeping the workflow close to the engineer.

## What is included

- A Python command-line interface for repeatable analysis.
- A Fusion 360 add-in for CAD-integrated workflows.
- A public, documented codebase that can continue to evolve.

For the implementation and current documentation, visit the [project repository](https://github.com/diovandi/kst-rating-tool).
