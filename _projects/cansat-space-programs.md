Several projects connected my technical aerospace background with program development.

On DARE’s 22nd executive board, I conducted feasibility and financial analysis for a bid to serve as a launch provider for CanSat Luxembourg. The work required translating mission and operational requirements into a credible organizational and financial decision.

At Antarexxa Space Global, I later mentored the Bamantara EEPISAT student team on the technical requirements of the 2024 CanSat competition. I also contributed to a strategic roadmap for Indonesia’s amateur-rocketry sector and supported planning for a K–12 space-education curriculum.

These projects are less visible than a code repository or CAD assembly, but they represent the systems layer around engineering: matching technical requirements to team capability, identifying operational dependencies, structuring a program, and helping younger teams turn an ambitious mission into an executable plan.
