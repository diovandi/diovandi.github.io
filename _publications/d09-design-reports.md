This report series follows a large passenger-aircraft concept from requirements and sizing through detailed wingbox design.

The third work package finalized external geometry, Class II weight and drag estimates, and a converged configuration. The fourth established critical maneuver and gust load cases, force diagrams, stiffness requirements, and a preliminary wingbox. The fifth compared six layouts for buckling, tensile strength, crack propagation, and aileron reversal before selecting a final structural configuration.

My task records show contributions to:

- Aircraft design and Class II estimations
- Critical load-case analysis
- Aileron-reversal analysis
- Final wingbox design and technical figures
- CATIA flap-mechanism and wing-section work
- Report proofing, layout, references, and integration

These documents are design-course reports authored by a nine-person team. They are included as evidence of systems engineering and technical writing—not described as journal papers.
